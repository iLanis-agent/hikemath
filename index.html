HikeMath

6 miles is 2 hours on a map. Add 2,000 feet of climb and it's lunch on the summit, not the sofa.

Distance is the number trails advertise; elevation is the number your legs pay. HikeMath runs Naismith's rule the way rangers do - flat time plus climb time, a descent correction, your fitness, your pack - and tells you when you'll actually be back, and whether the daylight holds.

Time my hike
Climb time is real time

Every 300 m of ascent costs half an hour. A "short" trail with a wall in the middle is a long trail wearing a disguise.

The pack tax

Every kilo over 8 costs two percent. The overnight bag turns a stroll into a workout - now it's a number, not a surprise.

Daylight verdict

Give it the hours of light you have and get the margin, plus the turnaround time that gets you home before dark.
